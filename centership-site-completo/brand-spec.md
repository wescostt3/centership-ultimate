## Typography

- Display: technical sans, 600 weight, tight tracking for large headlines.
- Body: technical sans, 400 weight, direct and objective.
- Mono: metrics, labels, process steps and operational metadata.

## Layout posture

- Industrial premium: dark graphite base, concrete/steel surfaces and one amber accent.
- Low radius: 6-10px on controls and cards; no playful rounded shapes.
- Hairline borders, no heavy shadows; depth comes from contrast and photography.
- Strong grid: technical blocks, process lanes, service matrix and proof points.
- Accent budget: amber appears in CTA, process marks and small operational highlights only.

## Visual cues from source material

- Real operating environment: elevator shafts, metal beams, cabling, machinery and controlled demolition.
- Positioning should emphasize technical removal, planning, site discipline and risk reduction.
- Founder material may support trust when needed, but the brand should lead as CenterShip.
